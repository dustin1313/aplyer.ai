Your first 7 days after a layoff

A recruiter's honest checklist. Do these before sending a single application.

  • Update your resume. Change your most recent role from "present" to the end date. Add any recent accomplishments you haven't documented yet.
  • Run the Resume Red Flag Audit. Find out what a recruiter would flag before you start sending it out.
  • Update your LinkedIn headline by adding the "Open to Work" designation. LinkedIn's own data shows it increases recruiter messages by 40% (LinkedIn, 2025 via CNBC).
  • Reach out to former colleagues and managers to begin networking. A short, honest LinkedIn post reaches more people than you think.
  • Score your resume against 3 real job descriptions. Fix the gaps before applying.
  • Read the Job Scam Field Guide before responding to any unsolicited offers. Laid-off candidates are the number one target for job scams, and the fakes are getting harder to spot.
  • Set a daily routine. Ten quality applications per day beats fifty mass-applies. Answer the long written questions. Most candidates skip them.
