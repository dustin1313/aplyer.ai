Install it once, upload your resume once, then let it work where the problem actually happens: on the application page, while you are still on it.

Q.1

Why are Aplyer and you different?

We work inside job applications where you apply.
WORKDAY · GREENHOUSE · LEVER · APPLY FROM LINKEDIN, INDEED, OR ANYWHERE · MORE BEING ADDED
Every other tool assumes you have already decided to apply. They help you fill a form faster, but they make you leave the page, open another app, paste in job descriptions, and bring the work to them. Aplyer activates where the problem actually happens. You never leave the page. You never open another app. The moment you land on a careers page, Aplyer is already there.

And unlike every competitor, Aplyer answers application questions in your voice, not generic AI output that every recruiter recognizes instantly. Every time you edit an answer, Aplyer learns. 1 / SETUP
Upload your resume once
Drag and drop on install. Aplyer learns your tone, achievements, and writing style. Add writing samples for even better accuracy. Never upload again. Your voice memory starts building immediately.
2 / DETECT
Activates automatically, you never leave the page
Find the job on LinkedIn, Indeed, or any job board. Click apply. When the application page loads, Aplyer activates automatically on Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever. No switching to another app. No copying and pasting. You stay on the job page the entire time. More platforms being added.

Every question answered. Every field filled.
Application questions get answers in your voice using your resume. Standard fields like name, address, and work history fill automatically. Review everything, edit anything, hit submit.
After generating each answer, Aplyer checks it against an independent AI detector, the kind sold to recruiters and hiring managers, and shows you a Human Score. The higher the score, the more your answer reads like you wrote it. You review everything before it submits. You are always in control.
Q.2

Why should we hire Aplyer and you?

Aplyer reads the job description alongside your resume and writing samples when generating answers. It pulls the specific keywords, required skills, and language the employer used in the job posting and weaves them into your answers. This is how you get through ATS screening. The system is looking for exact keyword matches from the job description. A generic answer about leadership that does not use the employer's specific language scores poorly. An answer that mirrors their terminology scores highly. Aplyer does both simultaneously, it sounds like you and it speaks the language of that specific job.

Every application is logged automatically so you always know what was sent, can review every answer, and build a library of your strongest responses over time, as shown below.

Frequently Asked Questions +
Is this AI? +
Yes, Aplyer is powered by AI. But it is not generic AI. Aplyer uses WriteDNA Technology to learn how you specifically write from your resume and your own words. Every answer is unique to you because it is built from your voice, your experience, and your writing style. The result is an answer that reads like you sat down and wrote it yourself.
Is this like ChatGPT? +
No. ChatGPT gives everyone the same generic output from the same prompt. Aplyer uses WriteDNA Technology, our own proprietary system built specifically for job applications. It learns how you specifically write from your resume and your own words, which means every answer is different for every candidate. You cannot replicate this by opening ChatGPT and typing a prompt.
Will it get flagged? +
No. WriteDNA Technology writes each answer the way you would naturally type it, not copied and pasted from a chatbot. Every answer is verified against an independent AI detector before you see it. You review everything and can edit anything before submitting. Every application is in your hands.
