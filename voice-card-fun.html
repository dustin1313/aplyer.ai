Why this one travels and the recruiter version did not

Nothing on this card mentions jobs, applications, or resumes. It is about how the person writes and thinks, full stop. That is what makes it safe to post for someone keeping their search quiet, and it is what earns the caption you described: this thing is scary accurate, or, this is so not me, and either reaction gets a share.

The fortune-cookie principle: the lines are flattering, a little general, and open enough that the reader fills in the meaning. When one lands, it feels personal. When one misses, they laugh and share it anyway. Nobody expects an AI to know them perfectly, so the looseness is part of the fun, not a flaw.

The one honesty line to hold: playful and general is fine, a fabricated statistic is not. Say rare breed as obvious flavor, never top 3 percent as a fake measurement. WriteDNA picks the type from the real fingerprint, then says it in warm, loose language.
