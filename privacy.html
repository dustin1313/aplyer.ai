Privacy Policy

How Aplyer.ai Collects, Uses, and Protects Your Information

Aplyer LLC | Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Aplyer LLC (Aplyer, we, us, or our) operates the website Aplyer.ai and the Aplyer Chrome extension (collectively, the Service). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, what we refuse to collect, how we use it, your rights regarding that information, and how we protect it. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you join our waitlist by submitting your email address, create an account, upload your resume or paste writing samples, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, resume content, professional history, writing samples, and any other content you choose to provide.

Every writing sample is provided by pasting text. Aplyer does not connect to, read, or fetch content from LinkedIn, X, or any other social or professional network, and does not ask for handles or account access.

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Service we may automatically collect browser type and version, operating system, which pages or features you access, date and time of access, IP address, and referring URL. We use this to improve the Service and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell this data.

1.3 What the extension does on a job application page

When you use the Aplyer Chrome extension on a company careers page, the extension reads the visible text of the form fields on that page, including question text and the job description, in order to generate answers, fill fields from your resume, and score your resume match. Application question text is processed in real time and is not retained by Aplyer servers after the answer is generated. Job description text may be cached in hashed form for up to 30 days so that scoring is consistent across sessions.

Fields we fill from your resume. Employer, job title, job location, employment dates, duties and bullet points, school, degree, field of study, education dates, skills, certifications, total years of experience, and your name, email, phone, location and profile links. These come from the resume you uploaded. You can edit every one of them before you submit.

Fields we remember only after you answer them yourself. Willingness to relocate, willingness to travel, and earliest start date. We store these only after you have typed or selected them on a real application, never before, and never by inferring them from anything.

Fields we leave alone, always. See Section 2.

1.4 Sites we do not yet support

Aplyer currently works on applications hosted by Workday, Greenhouse and Lever. If you press the send-it-in control after opening Aplyer on a careers page we do not support, we record the domain of that site so we know which platforms to build next. We record the domain only, never the full web address and never the job you were looking at, and only at the moment you press it.

Once we have the domain, we may look up publicly available information about that site from our own servers, such as which application platform it runs on, so we can group requests by platform. That lookup uses the public careers page and public domain records. It involves none of your information and happens on our systems, not in your browser.

We do not store which account sent it. So that one person pressing it repeatedly does not look like many people, we store a one-way hash combining your account identifier with that domain. The hash lets us count how many different people have run into a given site. It cannot be reversed, and it cannot be used to produce a list of the sites you have visited. We do not record the pages you visit and nothing is recorded unless you actively press the control.

2. What We Never Collect

This section is a commitment, not a description of current practice that might change quietly. If it changes, we will tell you before it does.

Demographic information. We do not collect, store, or fill your race, ethnicity, gender, veteran status, or disability status. This includes the decline-to-answer option. Where an application asks these questions, Aplyer leaves the fields untouched and you answer them yourself or not at all.

Legal eligibility answers. We do not collect, store, or fill your work authorization status, visa sponsorship needs, criminal or conviction history, background check consent, drug test consent, or security clearance. These are statements you make to an employer about yourself, and Aplyer never makes them for you, never remembers them, and never derives them from your resume.

Salary expectations. We do not store any salary figure you enter. You write your own number on every application.

Identity and financial identifiers. We do not collect Social Security numbers, national insurance or equivalent numbers, passport numbers, driver licence numbers, government ID numbers, tax identification numbers, bank account or routing numbers, or payment card numbers. If your resume contains any of these, they are stripped before storage. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe and card numbers never reach our servers.

Dates of birth. We do not collect or store your date of birth.

Browsing activity. The extension reads page content only on pages where you use it. It does not collect your browsing history, your keystrokes, or content from pages unrelated to a job application. It runs nothing in the background and records nothing about a page unless you open Aplyer on it. The single exception is the unsupported-site domain described in Section 1.4, which is recorded only when you click the Aplyer icon.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain and improve the Service; to generate answers using your resume and voice profile; to send you waitlist updates, early access notifications and product announcements; to respond to your inquiries; to comply with legal obligations; and to detect and prevent fraud, abuse or security incidents.

We will never sell your personal information. We will never use your resume content or writing samples to train AI models beyond providing the Service to you. We may use anonymized, aggregated patterns from how users edit generated answers to improve our technology. Where we do, that data is never tied to your identity and cannot be used to identify you. No individual writing sample, resume, or generated answer is shared with, sold to, or used to produce output for another user.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on these bases: contract, where processing is necessary to provide the Service you requested; legitimate interests, for analytics and security that do not override your rights; consent, where you have given it, such as joining the waitlist; and legal obligation, where processing is required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Resume content and writing samples are retained while your account is active and are deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request. The original resume file you upload is deleted immediately after the text is extracted from it. We retain the extracted text, not the document.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct inaccurate or incomplete data, to request deletion, to receive your data in a machine-readable format, to object to processing for certain purposes, and to request that we restrict how we process your data. To exercise any of these, email privacy@aplyer.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, email privacy@aplyer.ai.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted transmission using TLS, encryption at rest, per-user isolation enforced at the database level so that one user record is never readable by another, and limited access to personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following processors. Each has its own privacy policy. We share only the minimum data necessary.

  • Anthropic. AI model provider. Receives resume excerpts, pasted writing samples, and application question text in order to generate answers.
  • OpenAI. AI model provider, used only in the comparison demo on our website. If you use that demo and provide a resume and a job description, that text is also sent to OpenAI so we can show you what an ordinary AI answer looks like next to an Aplyer one. The Aplyer Chrome extension never sends anything to OpenAI.
  • Supabase. Database and authentication. Stores your account, resume text, writing samples, voice profile and generated answers, encrypted at rest and isolated per user.
  • Vercel. Application programming interface hosting. Processes requests between the extension and the model provider.
  • GitHub Pages. Static hosting for the Aplyer.ai website and the free tool pages. Receives no user account data.
  • Copyleaks and Pangram. AI detection services. Receive generated answer text only, not personal information.
  • Brevo. Email delivery. Receives your email address for waitlist and product communications.
  • ZeroBounce. Email validation. Receives your email address to verify that it is deliverable.
  • Stripe. Payment processing. Receives payment information directly. Card numbers never touch Aplyer servers.

We do not share data with any third party beyond what is necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. Separately, our Terms of Service require all users to be at least 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on the Service. If we ever intend to change anything in Section 2, we will tell you before the change takes effect, not after.
