Questions I get asked
Am I paying anything today?
No. Joining the waitlist is free and takes an email address. You will see the price and the start date before anything is charged.
Why is there a free tier at all?
Because you should get to watch Aplyer answer a real question on your own resume before you decide anything.
Do I need a credit card to join?
No. Just an email address.
What happens when I get hired?
You cancel. A job search ends, and a subscription that outlives it is one you should not be paying for.
Does Aplyer apply to jobs for me?
No. It fills the form and you read it, change whatever you want, and press submit yourself. Nothing is sent without you.
Will this get me an interview?
No tool can promise that. What Aplyer changes is your odds. 57% of candidates have abandoned an application because it was too long (LiveCareer, 2025), so the jobs with written questions draw the smallest pools. Finish one and you are competing against a fraction of the people who clicked LinkedIn Easy Apply, with answers in your voice rather than the generic AI output a recruiter spots in two seconds.
