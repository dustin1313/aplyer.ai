Your first 7 days after a layoff

A recruiter's honest checklist. Do these before sending a single application.

  • Update your resume. Remove the company that just let you go from "present" and change it to the end date. Add any recent accomplishments you have not documented yet.
  • Run the Resume Red Flag Audit. Find out what a recruiter would flag before you start sending it out.
  • File for unemployment. Do this on day one. There is no reason to wait and there is zero stigma.
  • Update your LinkedIn headline. Add "Open to Work" if you are comfortable with it. LinkedIn's own data shows it increases recruiter outreach by 40%.
  • Identify 10 target companies. Not job listings. Companies. Research who is hiring in your field and make a list before you start applying.
  • Score your resume against 3 real job descriptions using Resume Score vs the Job. Fix the gaps before applying.
  • Set a daily application routine. Two quality applications per day beats twenty mass-applies. Answer the long written application questions. Most candidates skip them, which is exactly why they work.
  • Tell your network. A short, honest LinkedIn post reaches more people than you think. People want to help. Let them.

You have enough to worry about. Applications should not be one of them.
